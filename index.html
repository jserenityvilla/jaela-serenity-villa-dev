    Appliance Guide

    Please follow these simple instructions when using the appliances during your stay.

    📺 Smart TV

    Use the remote control to switch on the TV and enjoy YouTube and other streaming services.

    ❄️ Air Conditioner

    Set the temperature to around 24°C and keep doors and windows closed while operating.

    🍳 Cooker & Oven

    Always turn off the gas cooktop and oven after cooking. Never leave cooking unattended.

    🧺 Washing Machine

    Load clothes evenly, add detergent and choose the appropriate wash program.

    ☕ Electric Kettle

    Fill with water only, avoid overfilling and switch off after use.

    🧊 Refrigerator

    Please keep the door closed and avoid changing the temperature settings.

    ⚠ Appliance Care

    Please switch off all appliances when leaving the villa. If you experience any problems, contact the host for assistance.

    Kitchen

    Our fully equipped kitchen has everything you need to prepare meals during your stay.

    🍳 Cooking Appliances

    Gas cooktop, electric oven, microwave, rice cooker, toaster and electric kettle.

    🍽️ Cookware & Utensils

    Pots, pans, knives, chopping board, cooking utensils, plates, bowls, cups and glasses.

    ☕ Complimentary Items

    Tea, coffee, sugar, salt and pepper are provided for your convenience.

    🚰 Drinking Water

    Filtered drinking water is available. Please use this water for drinking and making beverages.

    🧹 Kitchen Care

    Wash dishes after use, wipe benches clean and dispose of rubbish in the bins provided.

    🔥 Safety Tips

    Turn off the gas after cooking, never leave cooking unattended and supervise children.

    💚 Before You Leave the Kitchen

    Please switch off all appliances, clean used dishes and leave the kitchen as you found it.
